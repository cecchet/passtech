Getting through tech with PassTech

Three ways to use the racer safety-equipment checker — by Frog Racing

PassTech checks your driver gear — helmet, balaclava, HANS/HNR, firesuit, gloves, shoes, undergarment, arm restraint — and your car gear — seat, belts/harness, window net, fuel cell, and more — against the published rules of 36 rulesets. This is the same guided tour built into the app — tap “How it works” any time to run it yourself, right down to picking a body, refining by class, and opening the rulebook.

1

What does a body require?

No gear entry needed. Pick a sanctioning body and browse exactly what it requires, what it accepts, and any conditions — a reference copy of the rulebook, not a check against your own equipment.

  1. From the main menu, choose “Check the rules”
  2. Toggle Driver Safety Gear / Car Safety Gear at the top to focus on just one section — both are on by default, and a Rollover protection section is coming soon.
  3. Pick a body and discipline from the dropdown, e.g. SCCA Time Trial — PHA.
  4. Each category shows its requirement, accepted certifications, and any conditions — straight from the rulebook.
  5. Standards the body doesn’t list are collapsed under a red “Not accepted” panel, so gaps in a rulebook are easy to spot.

2

Will my equipment pass tech?

Enter what you actually have, checked against one body’s rules. Each item resolves independently, and an overall PassTech verdict sits at the top — green, yellow, or red.

  1. Choose “Will my equipment pass tech?” and pick a body.
  2. For each item, choose I have this item or I don’t have this item — the box expands automatically.
  3. Pick the certification standard printed on the tag, or use Scan tag photo to read it from a picture.
  4. The verdict banner updates live: red for a real violation, yellow when only an optional/conditional item is unresolved, green once everything required checks out.

Verdict states

Fail — Helmet: required, not entered.
Conditional — Shoes: no cert needed, not specified.
Pass — everything required is satisfied.
3

Where can my equipment race?

Enter your gear once, and PassTech checks it against every ruleset it knows — useful if you run with more than one club or series. Results sort into three buckets.

  1. Choose “Where can my equipment race?” and enter your gear (no body to pick — it checks all of them).
  2. Bodies you fully satisfy land in Eligible.
  3. Eligible under condition means everything required is fine, but an optional item (e.g. an arm restraint that only applies to open cars) is still unresolved.
  4. Does not meet the requirements covers anything missing or rejected outright.

Example split (36 rulesets)

Eligible (1) — CARS Rally Cross
Eligible under condition (4) — SCCA Solo, SCCA RallyCross…
Does not meet the requirements (31) — SCCA Road Racing, NASA…

Also worth knowing

📷 Scan instead of typing

On any item, use Scan tag photo to snap the certification tag — it reads the standard and any printed dates, and flags photos that look like the wrong kind of tag.

⚠️ Expiration warnings

An accepted certification expiring within the current calendar year shows a warning with the exact date, right next to that item’s result.

📲 Works offline

From the main menu, install PassTech to your home screen or desktop — it keeps working at the track with no signal.

🎯 Refine by class

When a body defines its own competitor classes — AMEC’s Street Legal vs. Super Modified, SCCA Solo’s Street vs. Modified, an SCCA GCR class exemption — a Refine by class dropdown appears under the body picker. Pick yours and the rules sharpen to exactly your class; leave it on “All classes” for the general picture.

PassTech is a pre-screening tool, not a certification — the tech inspector still makes the final call. passtech.frogracing.us
